VOID Privacy Policy

Effective May 1, 2026

Overview

VOID is the hub that connects experiences. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you access VOID.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: username, email, and authentication credentials.
  • Usage signals: device, browser, and session metadata for security and performance.
  • Optional profile data you choose to provide.

How We Use Data

  • To authenticate and keep your account secure.
  • To operate and improve VOID services.
  • To meet legal, safety, and compliance obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share only with trusted providers to deliver services, or when required by law.

Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact support for assistance.
