Support & FAQ

Quick answers + a way to contact support without leaving the app.

Why can’t I message someone?

You can only message users who accepted your contact request. Open Chats → Menu → Requests to accept or resend.

Are messages encrypted?

Messages are encrypted at rest in the database (server-side) so raw DB content isn’t readable. This is not end-to-end encryption between devices.

How do profile photos work?

Your photo is stored on the server under static/avatars/ and referenced from your account record.

How do I change my name/about/password?

In Chats open Menu → Settings. Edit Profile changes your display name/about. Your Account lets you update email/password or delete your account.
