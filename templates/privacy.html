Privacy Policy

How LinkUp handles your information.

Summary

This is a basic policy template you can customize.

LinkUp stores your account details (username, email) and your messages in the app database. Messages are encrypted at rest by this app.

What we collect

  • Account data: username, email, and hashed password.
  • Profile data you add: display name, about, avatar.
  • Chat content you send (stored encrypted at rest).
  • Files you upload as chat attachments.

How we use it

  • To provide the chat service (deliver messages, store your profile).
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile in the app.
  • You can delete your account from the account menu (if enabled in your build).
