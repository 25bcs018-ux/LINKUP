Privacy Policy

How LinkUp handles your information.

Summary

We collect only what is needed to run LinkUp and keep it secure.

LinkUp stores your account details and messages in the app database. Messages are encrypted at rest by this app but are not end-to-end encrypted.

Information we collect

  • Account data: username, email, and hashed password.
  • Profile data you provide: display name, about text, and avatar.
  • Chat content you send and receive (stored encrypted at rest).
  • Files you upload as chat attachments.
  • Basic usage and security logs (for abuse prevention and debugging).

How we use information

  • Provide the chat service and deliver messages.
  • Maintain account security and prevent abuse.
  • Improve reliability and performance.

Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We share data only with service providers needed to operate LinkUp.

Retention

  • We keep data for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the service.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account where this is supported in the app.

Contact

  • For privacy questions, use the in-app support page.
