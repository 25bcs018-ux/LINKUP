The Void Manual

A living guide to the hub, the apps, and how everything moves together.

What is VOID?

VOID is the body. Every app is a part of it. You enter the body once, then choose the part that fits your moment. It is a hub that keeps your identity, privacy, and momentum in one place.

Think of VOID as a control room for human connection, creation, and secure movement across experiences.

The Objectives of VOID

  • Unify identity so you sign in once and move everywhere.
  • Reduce friction so switching apps feels instant.
  • Keep privacy first with clear boundaries between apps.
  • Make every experience feel guided and familiar.
  • Keep support and feedback in one place.

Privacy in VOID

VOID treats privacy like a core system, not a setting. Every app inherits secure foundations and stays isolated so your data is scoped to the space you are using.

  • Identity is centralized, but data is segmented by app.
  • Access is explicit: you choose where you enter and what you share.
  • Security is visible, not hidden. You can see how to sign out and revoke access quickly.

Familiarity and Flow

VOID is designed to feel like a place you can return to. Every app uses shared patterns so you never relearn the basics.

  • One hub, one identity, one rhythm.
  • Consistent navigation and consistent support.
  • Shortcuts like the app switcher keep your flow fast.

How to Operate VOID

  1. Enter the hub and scan the apps on the left.
  2. Hover a card to preview. Click to launch.
  3. Open the account drawer for profile, usage, and support.
  4. Use the app switcher button to jump between apps at any time.
  5. Return to the hub whenever you want a full reset.

Navigation updates

  • The app drawer now supports smoother slide-in motion.
  • Hub navigation flow has been refined for faster switching.

LinkUp

LinkUp is the core messaging and presence layer. It is designed to feel human and safe.

Why it exists

To reduce noise and make conversation intentional. No random DMs. No chaos.

How it works

  • Contact requests are required before direct messages.
  • Messages are encrypted at rest in storage.
  • Groups use majority voting for membership changes.
  • NOVA assistant can help inside chats.

How to use it

  • Send a contact request, wait for acceptance, then chat.
  • Create a group and invite members. Votes handle changes.
  • Open NOVA from the chat interface for guidance.

Secure

Secure is the private vault side of VOID. It is where sealed conversations and protected content live.

Why it exists

To give you a space where privacy is the default, not a feature.

How it works

  • Private spaces are isolated from the rest of the system.
  • Access is deliberate. You choose who can enter.
  • Content is treated as sensitive by default.

How to use it

  • Enter Secure for protected chats or vaults.
  • Keep sensitive threads here instead of open spaces.
  • Exit Secure when you are done to return to the hub.

Kernel

Kernel is the control and operations layer. It helps you understand and manage what is running inside VOID.

Why it exists

To give you visibility and control over your environment.

How it works

  • Status views show system and workspace activity.
  • Operator tools keep your tasks organized.
  • Shortcuts reduce the time it takes to move between tools.

How to use it

  • Open Kernel when you want control panels and system views.
  • Use it as your command bridge before switching apps.

Creator

Creator is your studio inside VOID. It is designed for building, designing, and shaping content.

Why it exists

To move from idea to output without leaving the VOID ecosystem.

How it works

  • Workspace tools focus on design and media flow.
  • Projects keep your work organized.
  • Templates and assets speed up creation.

How to use it

  • Open Creator when you want to make or publish.
  • Start a project, add assets, and iterate.

Games

Games is the play space. It is connected to VOID so your identity follows you, but play stays playful.

Why it exists

To keep a lightweight space for experiments and arcade experiences.

How to use it

  • Open Games to explore mini experiences.
  • Return to the hub whenever you want to switch modes.

Current lineup

  • PASS_GEN (password guesser).
  • Snake (classic arcade).
